Japanese Kanji

Dive into the complex and intriguing world of Kanji, the logographic characters used in the Japanese writing system. Learn about their history, meanings, and how to read and write them effectively.

What is Kanji?

Kanji are characters that originated from Chinese writing and were adapted into the Japanese language. Each Kanji character represents a word or a meaningful part of a word, often conveying complex ideas in a single symbol.

Common Kanji Characters

  • 日 (にち / に / ひ) - Sun, day
  • 月 (げつ / がつ / つき) - Moon, month
  • 山 (さん / やま) - Mountain
  • 川 (せん / かわ) - River
  • 人 (じん / にん / ひと) - Person
